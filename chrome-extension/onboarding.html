IngredientIQ installed

You'll now see ingredient safety warnings when logging food in supported apps.

1

Open a supported nutrition app

IngredientIQ activates automatically on:

MyFitnessPal MacrosFirst Cronometer Lose It!
2

Log a food as normal

When you tap "Add to Diary" or "Log Food," IngredientIQ checks the ingredients before proceeding.

3

See the safety warning

If flagged ingredients are found, a warning appears. You can log anyway, find an alternative, or dismiss.

Banned — FDA authorization revoked
Big 9 allergen — FSIS mandatory declaration
High concern — probable carcinogens, endocrine disruptors
Under FDA review — 2025 post-market reassessment
Secret GRAS — self-affirmed safe without FDA review
4

Optional: Add your Anthropic API key

Click the IngredientIQ icon in your browser toolbar → paste your Anthropic API key. This enables lookup for foods not in the local database (40 ingredients included without a key).

Data sources: Classifications are based on FDA 21 CFR, FSIS Directive 7230.1 (Jan 2024), and EWG Secret GRAS report (March 2026). This extension is not a substitute for medical advice. Always consult a healthcare provider for dietary decisions.
